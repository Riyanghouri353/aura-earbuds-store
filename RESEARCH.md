# Research behind AURA — 30 stores studied (10 Nov 2026)

The store was designed after a live study of 30 earbuds / audio stores in three groups of ten.

## Global flagships (Apple-style design rules)
Apple Store, Samsung, Sony, Bose, Nothing, OnePlus, Xiaomi, JBL, Anker Soundcore, Beats.
Taken: one idea per screen, giant tight-tracked headlines, borderless cards, sticky frosted nav plus a sticky product sub-nav with price + Buy, colour swatches as the only colour, honest compare-inside-the-buy-flow (Apple), one-stat-per-screen chapters (Xiaomi), delivery estimate printed early (Sony/Soundcore), certification proof in the hero (Soundcore), "why buy direct" trust strip (Bose).

## Pakistani stores (the trust layer)
Daraz, PriceOye, Telemart, Shophive, iShopping, HomeShopping, Audionic, Ronin, Zero Lifestyle, Dany Technologies (+ Mistore.pk checked as a bonus).
Taken: COD as the hero promise, PKR anchor pricing with strikethrough and % off, free-delivery threshold, city-tier delivery times (TCS/Leopards), JazzCash + Easypaisa as first-class payments, per-product installment line, 1-year warranty + 7-day replacement badges under the price, WhatsApp ordering and support, Roman-Urdu warmth in reviews and microcopy, price-band shopping (Under Rs 5,000 etc.), and the lesson from Telemart/HomeShopping that demanding advance payment destroys trust.

## DTC audio brands (product-page conversion)
boAt, Noise, Skullcandy, Sennheiser, Google Pixel Buds, Huawei, OPPO, realme, CMF by Nothing, Raycon.
Taken: title = product + ANC dB + battery hours, 4-icon spec strip above the fold, ANC on/off battery honesty, grouped spec table with lab footnotes, sibling comparison tables, review volume with a few honest 4-star reviews, bundle/upsell in the buy box, sticky mobile add-to-cart, FAQ accordions that answer warranty and delivery in Pakistan, price tiers Rs 3,499–7,999 (volume), Rs 9,999–16,999 (margin), Rs 24,999–34,999 (halo).

Full per-store notes are kept with the project owner.
